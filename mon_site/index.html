About

Econometrician with a focus on time series, causal inference and macro-finance.

I am a PhD candidate in econometrics with research interests spanning time series econometrics, monetary policy and systemic risk. My work combines traditional econometric models with modern tools from machine learning and deep learning.

I enjoy building bridges between theory and data: designing models that remain interpretable for economists while being flexible enough to capture complex dynamics in high-dimensional datasets.

Affiliation University of Montpellier · MRE
Fields Econometrics, Macro-finance, Time series
Methods Bayesian, ML, Deep learning

Forward guidance, systemic risk and nonlinear time series

Measuring the impact of ECB forward guidance on systemic stress indicators using nonlinear time-series models and high-frequency financial data.

  • Systemic risk indices (CISS)
  • Identification of policy shocks
